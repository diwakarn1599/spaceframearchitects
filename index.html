Our Story

Architecture That
Breathes & Endures

Est. 2015

Space Frame Architects is a Chennai-based design studio founded on the belief that great architecture tells a story. We blend contemporary design sensibilities with the rich spatial traditions of Tamil Nadu — creating structures that breathe, inspire, and endure.

From the courtyard vernacular of Chettinad mansions to the bold lines of OMR's tech corridors, our work is shaped by place — always rooted, always forward-looking.
