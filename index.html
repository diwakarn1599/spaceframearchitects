Approval

From Patta verification to final occupancy, we handle every statutory approval for you. Clear paperwork, correct documentation and timely follow-ups, so your project stays legally sound and on schedule.

Approval
  • Patta & Chitta Verification
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC)
  • Building Plan Approval (DTCP / CMDA)
  • Layout & Sub-division Approval
  • RERA Registration
  • Completion & Occupancy Certificate
